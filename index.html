Before You Go

Payment

Cash is preferred, especially small bills. Checks require ID. Cards/tap may include a small fee.

Medication Pickup

Pets must have been seen within the last year, or have current eligible rabies records.

Bring Records

Bring medication packaging or a previous receipt when possible to simplify the visit.

Save Paperwork

Vaccination paperwork is proof of service. Photograph or scan records for backup.
